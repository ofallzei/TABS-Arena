# TABS Arena Guide - v1.1.6
(Available languages in the app: English, Spanish, Russian, and Chinese.)
## How To Install
1. Go to **Releases** on the right side of the GitHub repository and open the latest release.
<img width="219" height="98" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/9ecd4bc1-c8c9-49cc-8b12-fa8f4ba439e8" />
2. Scroll down and download the ZIP file for the latest version.
<img width="1211" height="184" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/bf25e08f-277d-4068-b59f-5179b3f33c09" />
3. Extract the ZIP file anywhere on your PC.
4. Run `TABS.exe`.
### Updating To The Latest Version
Download and extract the latest version folder. You can delete the older application folder afterward.
Do not delete the TABS Arena folder inside AppData unless you also want to delete your saved matches and settings.
Important: Read the latest release notes before updating. Larger updates may make older saved games incompatible.

## Setup Before Playing
1. It is strongly recommended that you download and subscribe to all workshop maps and factions involving the name `Fallz`.
Factions:
- NEW UNITS | FALLZ
- NEW UNITS 2 | FALLZ
- REBALANCED | FALLZ
Maps:
- 2v2 Floating Arena | Fallz
- 2v2 Bridges | Fallz
- 2v2 More Open | Fallz
- 2V2 | Fallz
<img width="1016" height="278" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/3b309227-142d-46ee-9b28-8a040d3dab5a" />
<img width="1362" height="273" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/6afdeeae-87ea-4fc7-9db9-a52cb6a30d85" />
2. Read the workshop descriptions because some maps have special instructions.
3. At least one player must own TABS on Steam and act as the host.
4. Other players do not need to own TABS, but they must have Steam installed.
5. The host should open TABS and press `Shift + Tab` to open the Steam overlay.
6. Select `Remote Play Together`.
7. Invite the other players with a Steam friend invite or the provided link.
8. Give the other players mouse and keyboard access, or controller access, through Remote Play Together.
9. Once everyone is connected, enter Sandbox and begin playing.

## How To Play
<img width="1021" height="525" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/048bbedb-bc72-4a79-8515-0d3c70c5abe8" />
When the app opens, select either `1v1` or `2v2`.
The app defaults to:
- FT20 Mode
- Faction Mode ON
You may switch to FT13 or FT30 and may turn Faction Mode off before the match begins.
After choosing a format, enter the player names and press `Set`.
Every player starts with `1200 gold`.
When Faction Mode is enabled, every player starts with `3 random factions`.
At the start of a match, choose which player or team acts first:
- In 1v1, the first player receives `+50 gold`.
- In 2v2, each player on the first team receives `+40 gold`.
### Turn Order
Round 1 follows the first-turn selection.
Afterward, the player or team with more points acts first.
If the score is tied, the player or team that won the latest round acts first.
During a turn, players may buy upgrades, buy troops, move units, sell units, or use other available shop options.
